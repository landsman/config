# Brain

My long-term notes live in `~/projects/landsman/brain`, a private repo: `raw/`
for sources as they arrived, `wiki/` for pages compiled from them. Its own
`AGENTS.md` holds the conventions; read it before writing there.

- **Before researching something, look there first** —
  `grep -ril <term> ~/projects/landsman/brain/wiki`. A finding I already paid
  for should not be paid for twice.
- **When I say "remember this", "save it to my brain" or similar**, it goes there,
  not into the per-project agent memory — that one is scoped to a single repo
  and invisible to every other agent.
- Nothing from it goes into a public repo, commit or doc; see
  [where the repos live](where-repos-live.md).
